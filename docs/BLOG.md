## Adding an article

1. Copy an existing article in `site/blog/` and rename it with a short, hyphenated slug, for example `deposits.html`.
2. Update the `<title>`, `<meta name="description">` and the Open Graph tags in `<head>`.
3. Write the article in the body. Keep the same structure: a one-line answer up top, short sections, a closing call to action.
4. Add a card for it in `site/blog/index.html` with the right topic filter.
5. Optionally add it to the blog section on the homepage.
6. Push. It's live at `aystoai.com/blog/<slug>`.

## Writing rules

- One question per article, answered in the first two lines.
- UK spelling and UK rules (ASA and CAP for advertising, PECR for marketing messages).
- Real numbers where you have them. Say where they come from.
- 600 to 1,200 words. Clinic owners read these between patients.
- End with one clear next step, usually the free call.
