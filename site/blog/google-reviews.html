Reviews

How to get more Google reviews without it feeling awkward

Reviews are often the first thing a new patient checks. When to ask, how to make it one tap, and what to do about the odd bad one.

Samuel Ayo, Founder of AYSTO · 12 October 2026 · 3 min read

Reviews are often the first thing a new patient checks before they get in touch. Most happy patients are glad to leave one. They just need to be asked at the right moment, in a way that takes ten seconds.

Ask at the right time

For a lot of treatments, the best moment isn't when they walk out. It's when the results have settled and they're pleased. A short follow-up message at that point works far better than asking at the till.

Make it one tap

Send a direct link to your Google review page. Every extra step loses people.

Ask everyone, and don't offer rewards

Google's rules don't allow you to only ask the patients you think are happy, or to offer discounts in return for reviews. Ask everyone the same way. It's fairer, and your reviews will be more believable.

Reply to every review

A short thank you on the good ones. On the odd bad one, stay calm, don't confirm any treatment details in public, and invite them to contact the clinic directly.

Put it on autopilot

The ask is easy to forget on a busy week. Set it to go out automatically after each appointment, at the right time for each treatment.
