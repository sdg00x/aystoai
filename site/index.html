FAQ

Questions clinics ask.

Anything else, just ask on the call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your clinic needs. After the free call you get one fixed price, agreed before any work starts, and you can spread it over 3 monthly payments. Most clinics only need to win back a handful of patients for it to pay for itself.

Will the AI give medical advice?

No. It answers questions about treatments, prices and availability. Anything clinical is noted and passed to your team, and you decide exactly what it can and can't say.

Is patient data safe?

We build with UK GDPR in mind, only collect what's needed to book, and use secure, reputable providers. We can put a data processing agreement in place with your clinic.

Will it work with my booking software?

We connect to your booking system wherever it allows. If it doesn't, we set up the next best thing, like a calendar your team already checks.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. Have a listen to the call at the top of the page. We train it on how your clinic talks to patients, and it hands over to a real person whenever it should.

How long does it take?

Most clinics are live within a few weeks. We'll give you a firm date on the call.
