FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to provide content?

Not much. Your logo if you have one, a few photos and the basics about what you do. If you don't have the words, we'll write them with you on the call.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. You get two rounds of changes during the build, and you can ask for changes any time after. Small edits are covered by the care plan, and we'll quote for anything bigger before we start.

Who owns the site?

You do. The site, the domain and the content are all in your name.

What if I don't like the mockup?

Then you don't pay anything. We can have another go, or you walk away. No hard feelings.

How do I pay?

Half when you've approved the mockup and we start building, and the other half when your site is live and you're happy with it.

Are there any running costs?

Your domain costs a small amount each year, and we'll tell you exactly what it is before we start. Add-ons like the care plan are optional.
